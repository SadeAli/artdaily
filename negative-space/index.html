$ man negative-space

Each picture traps one pocket of paper between its shapes — that pocket is the negative space. It breathes twice, then goes quiet: trace its outline right on top of the picture and press done ✓. “show again” replays the breath without touching your trace, “undo” drops your last stroke, “clear” wipes them all.

Lifting is free. Pick the line up near where you put it down and the drill reads the pieces as one line, so a short-throw trackpad or a small phone screen costs you nothing.

Scoring compares your line with the true edge of the space both ways, so close and complete beats fast. The tolerance has a pixel floor and is eased for pen, mouse or finger — the HUD says which — so a small screen is not a stricter drill. Three spaces per round, slimmer each time; the first one of a fresh round stays tinted while you trace.

Studio tip: name nothing. If you catch yourself thinking “mug”, half-close your eyes until the detail drops away and the picture is only dark shapes and light holes again.
