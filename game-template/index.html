$ man game-slug

Tap the centre dot of each target. Five targets, then a score.

Closer is better: dead centre is 100, and the score fades to 0 the further out you land.

After every tap the target stays up with your mark on it and a line to the dot, so you can see which way you were off before the next one. The dotted ring is where the score runs out — land on it and that tap is worth nothing. (The drill says this once, on your first reveal; it is here for the second time you wonder.)

At the end of the round, if your taps all drifted the same way, it says so — and how far to aim the other way next round. That is the habit to fix, not any one tap.

Studio tip: aim with the whole arm, not the fingers — pin the eye on the dot and let the hand arrive.
