$ man warm-up

Three 25-second sets — loose circles, diagonal sweeps, figure eights. The clock starts on your first mark, and you can lift as often as you like: every stroke in the set counts.

Scored 60% on how much ink you moved (the ring fills with distance, never accuracy) and 40% on a deliberately loose shape check. No red pen lives here.

Warm-ups are for the arm, not the drawing — draw from the shoulder, keep the pen moving, and let them be ugly.
